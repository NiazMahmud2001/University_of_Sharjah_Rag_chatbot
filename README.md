# Nexly — A Student Assistant for University Docs

Nexly helps students quickly find clear, reliable answers from official university handbooks and curated website pages.

## What It Does
- Delivers instant answers grounded in official sources.
- Always shows source references and links.
- Summarizes long sections and clarifies confusing rules.
- Fast, friendly experience on desktop and mobile.

## Who It’s For
- Students looking for policy clarity and guidance.
- Advisors and staff needing quick references.

## How It Works (Plain English)
- Searches trusted documents and surfaces the most relevant sections.
- Presents concise summaries with direct citations to the source.
- No speculation — answers are based on published, authoritative content.

## Common Use Cases
- Registration, add/drop, and graduation requirements.
- Academic policies (attendance, misconduct, appeals).
- Fees, deadlines, forms, and contact information.

## Using Nexly
- Open the app and ask a question.
- Review the answer and its source references.
- Follow links to the document page when needed.
- Copy, save, or share answers.

## Content Integrity & Privacy
- Answers come from official, publicly available documents.
- No personal data is required to use the app.
- For critical decisions, verify with the official source or your advisor.

## Status & Roadmap
- Active development: improved search, richer citations, bookmarks, accessibility, localization.
- Planned: topic guides, handbook change alerts, enhanced mobile experience.
